# Founder Intelligence Reporting

## Purpose and inputs
Interpret deterministic current, prior-day, 7-day, and 30-day KPI snapshots plus Nodepad read-only state, findings, costs, experiments, agent runs, and founder queues.

## Method
1. Validate sources and compare like-for-like periods; never manufacture missing values.
2. Explain validation, customer pull, experiment engine, execution, and efficiency separately.
3. Extract trajectory, bottleneck, opportunity, movements, critique, attention, concerns, and measurable celebrations.
4. Produce 3 founder-only actions, 3 measurable celebrations, and 3 decisions with alternatives, evidence, counterevidence, cost of waiting, and recommendation.
5. Classify workforce output as useful outcome, maintenance, duplicate/redundant, or no material result. Costs without customer/evidence movement are agent theater.
6. Add opportunity seeds as unvalidated inference with the cheapest next test.

## Quality gates and failure conditions
Numbers must come from deterministic input; every celebration has a delta; contact and decision items have provenance; insufficient inputs yield `insufficient_data`. Reject authoritative qualification fields and unsupported claims.

## Nodepad boundary
Reads context, hypotheses, evidence, experiments, and gaps. Proposes research gaps only. Never writes canonical qualification, confidence, scores, graph, evidence state, or decisions.

## Anti-patterns
Task-count vanity, treating prose as evidence, hiding contradictions, claiming causality from correlation, emailing or publishing without approval, and using LLM memory instead of immutable history.

# Customer Interview Question Bank

## Context & Role
- Can you tell me about your role and what you do on a typical day?
- How long have you been in this position/role?
- What are your main responsibilities?
- What does success look like in your role?

## Problem Exploration
- Tell me about the last time you encountered [problem area].
- What was happening when you realized this was an issue?
- What made it difficult or frustrating?
- How did you first become aware of this problem?
- How often does this happen?
- What have you tried to do about it so far?
- What hasn't worked and why?

## Current Solutions & Workarounds
- What solutions or tools are you currently using?
- What do you like about your current approach?
- What don't you like about it?
- Have you created any workarounds or shortcuts?
- What would make your current solution better?

## Ideal State & Needs
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would you change?
- What would your ideal experience look like?
- What would make this completely solved for you?
- What would you be able to do if this problem didn't exist?

## Decision Making & Change
- How do you decide when to try a new solution?
- What factors influence your decisions about new tools/methods?
- Who else is involved in these decisions?
- What would make you switch from what you're currently doing?
- What risks or concerns would prevent you from trying something new?

## Closing
- Is there anything else I should know about this topic?
- What questions do you have for me?
- Is there anyone else you think I should talk to about this?

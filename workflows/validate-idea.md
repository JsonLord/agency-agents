# Validate Idea Workflow

**Objective**: Validate that a problem worth solving exists and that customers are willing to pay for a solution.

## Steps

1. **Problem Exploration**
   - Agent: Problem Explorer
   - Output: Problem statement with evidence

2. **Customer Investigation**
   - Agent: Customer Investigator
   - Output: Validated problem with customer quotes and pain severity

3. **Assumption Mapping**
   - Skill: assumption-mapping
   - Output: Assumption map with desirability, viability, feasibility assumptions

4. **Assumption Validation**
   - Skill: assumption-validation-methods (applied to top-risk assumptions)
   - Output: Validation plan and evidence for critical assumptions

5. **Value Proposition Testing**
   - Agent: Value Proposition Designer (using customer language)
   - Output: Tested value proposition with customer feedback

6. **Willingness to Pay Test**
   - Skill: pricing-wtp
   - Output: Validated price range and revenue model

## Success Criteria

- Problem validated with at least 5 customer interviews showing clear pain
- At least 3 critical assumptions validated with evidence
- Value proposition tested with target audience (resonance > 4/5)
- Willingness to pay established with acceptable range

## Output

- Validated problem statement
- Assumption map with validation results
- Value proposition
- Pricing strategy

## Next Steps

If successful, proceed to investigate-hypothesis workflow.
If not, pivot or terminate.

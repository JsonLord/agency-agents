# Competitor Pain Mining Workflow

**Objective**: Mine competitor reviews and feedback to uncover customer pain points.

## Steps

1. **Competitor Identification**
   - Agent: Competitive Intelligence Analyst
   - Output: List of competitors to analyze

2. **Review Mining**
   - Skill: review-mining
   - Output: Competitor review data

3. **Pain Analysis**
   - Agent: Pain Miner (using pain-mining skill)
   - Output: Pain points from competitor reviews

4. **Pattern Identification**
   - Agent: Trend Scout
   - Output: Patterns in competitor customer pain

5. **Opportunity Synthesis**
   - Agent: Opportunity Synthesizer
   - Output: Opportunities to address competitor weaknesses

## Success Criteria

- Competitor reviews mined and analyzed
- Pain points identified and categorized
- Opportunities synthesized

## Output

- Competitor list
- Review mining data
- Pain points analysis
- Patterns
- Opportunities

## Next Steps

Use opportunities to inform hypothesis generation or value proposition design.
